How Equols works
1
Take the quiz
Answer 5 quick questions about how you experience the web. Equols builds a reading profile matched to you.
2
Your page adapts
On any website click Adapt page and Equols rewrites the content to match your profile — simpler words, better spacing, less clutter.
3
Fine-tune anytime
Use the Adjust tab to change any setting. Your preferences are saved per site so every visit feels right.
💡
Pro tip
Save settings for a site using the button on the home screen — Equols will remember them every time you visit.
Accessibility statement
Our commitment to digital inclusion
🎯
WCAG 2.1 AA
Equols is built to meet Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.1 Level AA. The popup interface uses semantic HTML, proper heading hierarchy, keyboard navigation, and ARIA labels throughout.
👁
Designed for everyone
Equols serves users with dyslexia, ADHD, visual impairments, cognitive fatigue, motor difficulties, and sensory sensitivities — as well as anyone who simply wants a better reading experience.
🔒
Your privacy
All settings are stored locally on your device. Equols never collects, tracks, or shares your data. Word tooltips use the free Dictionary API — no personal information is sent.
📬
Report an issue
If you find an accessibility barrier in Equols itself please contact us at uxwithgauri@gmail.com — we treat these reports as high priority and aim to respond within 2 working days.
Terms of use
USE OF EQUOLS

Equols is a free Chrome extension provided as-is for personal use. By installing Equols you agree to these terms.

WHAT EQUOLS DOES

Equols modifies the visual presentation of web pages in your browser. It does not access, store, or transmit any page content to external servers except when you use the word tooltip feature (individual words sent to dictionaryapi.dev) or the AI summarisation feature (page text sent to Anthropic API using your own API key).

YOUR DATA

No personal data is collected. All preferences are stored locally on your device using Chrome storage. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

Equols is provided without warranty. We are not liable for any issues arising from use of the extension including changes to page appearance or functionality.
