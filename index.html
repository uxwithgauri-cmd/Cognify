Cognify Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

Cognify does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers.

All user preferences are stored locally on your device using Chrome built-in storage. This data never leaves your browser.

Cognify does not track browsing history, collect analytics, or share data with third parties.

When using AI summarisation, webpage text is sent to the Anthropic API to generate a summary only. No identifying information is included and no data is stored.
